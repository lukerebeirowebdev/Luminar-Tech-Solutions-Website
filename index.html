FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you're after? Send a message using the form below.

How quickly can you fix my device? +

Most diagnostics are completed the same day, with many straightforward repairs — like virus removal or a Windows install — turned around within 24–48 hours.

Do you offer home visits or is it drop-off only? +

Both. I offer local home and business visits for on-site support, plus a drop-off option for repairs that need bench time.

Will I lose my files during a repair? +

Protecting your data is a priority. Where possible, files are backed up before any work begins, and data recovery is available if a drive has already failed.

Do you support Mac as well as Windows? +

Yes — with a background supporting Windows PCs, Macs and mobile devices in enterprise environments, both platforms are covered.

Can you support my small business ongoing, not just one-off repairs? +

Yes, ongoing support plans are available covering Microsoft 365, device management and network troubleshooting. Get in touch for pricing.
